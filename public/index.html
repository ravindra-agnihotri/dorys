FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering cakes in Baner Pune

Does Dory's Bakehouse deliver cakes in Baner Pune?

Yes! We deliver fresh cakes across Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Pashan and all nearby areas in Pune. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 12:00 noon.

Can I get an eggless cake? Are all flavours available eggless?

Absolutely! Every single flavour on our menu is available in eggless. We also offer vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free and sugar-free options. Just mention your preference when ordering.

How do I order a custom cake from Dory's Bakehouse?

Simply WhatsApp us at +91 91684 45014 with your design inspiration, cake size, flavour choice and the date you need it. We recommend ordering at least 1-2 days in advance for custom designs.

What flavours are available? How many flavours do you offer?

We offer 50+ handcrafted flavours including Chocolate Truffle, Black Forest, Red Velvet, Mango Delight, Strawberry, Tiramisu, Ferrero Rocher, Butterscotch, Oreo, Coffee Mocha and many more. All fruit fillings are made fresh with real fruits — no artificial flavours.

What is the minimum price for a cake? How much do cakes cost?

Our cakes start from ₹499 for a half-kg. Custom designer cakes, tier cakes and special theme cakes are priced based on design complexity and size. WhatsApp us for a free quote!

How far in advance should I order a birthday cake?

For standard cakes, same-day or next-day ordering is fine (before noon for same-day). For custom designer cakes, theme cakes, or large orders, we recommend 2-3 days in advance.
